# Paragraph examples

**Connected claims and evidence.** `Whether you're a dog person ... the British are well known as a nation of animal lovers. In the UK, 52% ... than without.` is ONE paragraph. Do **not** put a marker after `lovers.`; place `breakParagraph: true` on `without.` if another paragraph follows.

**Speaker change.** `Are you a dog person or a cat person, Neil?` and `Oh, I'm definitely a dog person ...` are separate speaker turns; mark the final `Neil?` word when there is a confirmed new speaker.

**WhisperX segment crossing.** If a topic runs through two or more segments, the first segment can end without a mark. Conversely, several paragraphs may occupy a single segment; place the marker on the relevant word.

**Sentence punctuation is not a paragraph.** Two sentences supporting the same claim remain together; punctuation alone never adds a mark. Pause duration is supporting evidence only.

**Cross-page continuity.** Page boundaries do not add a marker, restart first-line indentation, or create a new paragraph.
